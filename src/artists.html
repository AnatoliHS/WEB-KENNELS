Feminink Tattoos - Artist Portfolios

Explore the collected works of our talented all-female staff of tattoo artists

Faye

Tattoo Artist / Founder

As the founder of Feminink, Faye specializes in bold, custom designs that blend traditional techniques with a modern, inclusive aesthetic. Her vision for the studio was to create a safe, professional, and inspiring environment for both artists and clients alike.

Jaimie Jolly

Paramedical Tattooist & Permanent Cosmetics Tech

Jaimie is a specialist in paramedical tattooing and permanent cosmetics, helping clients feel confident and beautiful through expert restorative techniques. Her precision and dedication to the craft have made her a leader in aesthetic enhancement.

Zoe Vance

Junior Artist

Our talented junior artist, Zoe is passionate about fine-line work and minimalist designs, bringing a fresh perspective and meticulous detail to every piece. She is dedicated to creating subtle, meaningful art that resonates with her clients.

Samantha Leila

Tattoo Artist

Samantha excels in illustrative and neo-traditional styles, creating vibrant, storytelling pieces that are as unique as her clients. Her use of color and flow ensures that every tattoo is a dynamic work of art.

Emma Smith

Tattoo Artist

Emma's work focuses on delicate floral and geometric designs, with a mastery of shading and precision that results in timeless body art. Her approach is calm and focused, perfect for large, intricate pieces.
