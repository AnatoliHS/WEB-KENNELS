Róka's Story

Róka is bursting with life, she has a beautiful vibrancy about her in every way. Be it her shining personality, her eye-catching good looks or her graceful movement. She is incredibly in tune with her surroundings, and quick to learn new skills. She undoubtedly inherited her mother's intelligence and great problem-solving skills, as well as her father's working drive. Looking forward to exploring a variety of canine sports with this girl, including Agility, and Scent work for the purpose of conservation biology work.

Important note: Róka is registered with the ISSA as F1 foundation stock. She is part of an incredibly important biodiversity project to better the Shiloh Shepherd breed. (Read about it here) Due to the breeding restrictions on this line. I will be exceedingly selective about the placement of any breed-quality F2 progeny.
