F A Q

How is this course different from others?

Our WHMIS course is designed to provide comprehensive, easy-to-understand training that applies to a wide range of workplaces. Our program delivers clear, practical guidance that helps all workers identify and manage hazardous materials safely. This course focuses on:

  • Understanding Hazard Classes: Learn to recognize and interpret WHMIS symbols and labels across all types of controlled products.
  • Safety Data Sheets (SDS): Know how to read, access, and apply key information to protect yourself and your coworkers.
  • Safe Handling & Storage: Follow best practices for storing, using, and disposing of hazardous materials in any workplace.
  • Personal Protective Equipment (PPE): Understand how to select and properly use PPE for chemical, biological, or physical hazards.
  • Emergency Response: Be prepared for spills, exposures, and other incidents with clear steps for first aid and containment.

This course ensures that all workers, regardless of industry, receive the essential WHMIS training needed to maintain a safe and compliant workplace.


How long does it take?

The course typically takes around 60 minutes to complete, but the exact duration may vary depending on the individual. Some may finish more quickly, while others might take a bit longer to fully absorb the material.


Can I do a re-take exam?

Yes, you can retake the exam as many times as needed. There are unlimited attempts to pass the exam, so you can take it at your own pace until you're confident in your results.


When do i receive the certificate

Certificates are issued as soon as you pass the exam and will be sent to you via email.


How do we pay for the course?

The course can be purchased as an individual course or as a package if you are an employer buying courses for your employees. We accept all major credit cards for payment.


Does this course expire after a certain time of purchaise?

Once you purchase the course, you will have a full year to complete it at your own pace. This means you can start anytime within that period and progress through the material as your schedule allows. However, after one year from the purchase date, access to the course will expire.
