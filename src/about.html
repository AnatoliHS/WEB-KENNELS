About Us

Our Belleville Roots

Frustrated by the lack of inclusive, female-led tattoo spaces, our founder set out to build something different right here in Belleville. Feminink started as a dream to create a place free from judgment, where artistry and empathy lead.

We wanted to build a studio not just for women, but for anyone who has ever felt hesitant to step into a traditional tattoo parlour. Today, that dream is a thriving reality, built on community, creativity, and a whole lot of heart.

What We Stand For

What We Stand For

Inclusivity & Body Positivity

A Space for Every Body. We are a body-positive, LGBTQ+ affirming studio. Your identity is honored here, your pronouns are respected, and your story is safe with us.

Custom Artistry

Art with Intention. Your tattoo is a collaboration. We listen deeply to your ideas to ensure the final piece is a true reflection of you. Every design is crafted with care, skill, and passion.

Safety & Comfort

Your Well-being First. From single-use needles to hospital-grade sterilization and private rooms, we are meticulous about your safety and comfort from the moment you walk in.
