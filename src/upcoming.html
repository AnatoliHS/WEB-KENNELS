Do you enjoy canine sports like scent work, herding, or agility? Or being part of the breed's history? If you do, this might be the perfect litter for you! Wilderness Kennels is excited to announce the one-time pairing of Róka & Bapu! These pups will be F2 outcrosses meaning that these pups will be approximately 75% Shiloh Shepherd and 25% Belgian Shepherd.

Róka is affectionately referred to as the "Duchess of Chaos" at home. She enters the room with a clear royal vibrance about her, radiating grace in every movement. And man does this girl love to move! In traditional Tervuren fashion, she is perpetually in motion. She adores her agility classes and long walks in the back 40. At home, her favourite past times include keeping the Canadian geese off of the grass, beach walks, and playing with her pack mates. This past spring when raising the Mixal/Darko litter, I also got an opportunity to see an incredible window into Róka's parenting skills. She was so gentile and careful with her growing half-siblings and genuinely enjoyed having them around. This girl truly loves being in the presence of puppies.

Bapu resides in the Netherlands, bringing to the table a very traditional Shiloh look to this pairing with a wonderful stable temperament and a diverse mostly European Pedigree. He enjoys trotting off-leash alongside his owner's bike. He is fantastic at ignoring environmental distractions. He has a low-maintenance smooth coat, and lovely heavy bone structure, matched with effortless movement. He displays a lovely, friendly disposition. Bapu's mother Noa was also born in Ontario at Victory Shilohs some years ago, so it’s nice to have an opportunity to bring the line full circle since this Róka/Bapu litter will most likely be whelped and raised in British Columbia, Canada.

Pups are expected to arrive in both smooth and plush coats, with either Wolf Sable (Aw) like their father, or the eye-catching (Ay) Clear Sable of their mother. With a good possibility of beautiful light golden coats, with a small chance of Silvery greys like their father. Whites are not expected. But Bapu has not been DNA tested for coat colour genes, so one never really knows for sure.
