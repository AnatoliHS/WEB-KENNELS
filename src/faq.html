How is this course different from others?

Our WHMIS course for dental workers is tailored to address the unique hazards found in dental clinics. While standard WHMIS training covers general workplace hazardous materials, our dental-specific course focuses on:

  • Common Dental Hazards: Understanding exposure to disinfectants, sterilization chemicals, mercury from amalgam fillings, and nitrous oxide.
  • Biohazardous Risks: Proper handling of sharps, bloodborne pathogens, and infection control practices.
  • Specialized PPE: Training on personal protective equipment (PPE) tailored to dental professionals, including face shields, respirators, and chemical-resistant gloves.
  • Safe Handling & Storage: Guidance on the proper storage, use, and disposal of dental-specific chemicals like anesthetics and x-ray processing agents.
  • Emergency Procedures: Effective spill response and first aid protocols for chemical exposure in dental settings.

This course ensures that dental professionals receive the most relevant WHMIS training specific to their workplace environment.
