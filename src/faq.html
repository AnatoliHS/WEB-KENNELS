Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our process, pricing, and how we bring your digital vision to life. If you don't find your answer here, feel free to reach out.

Questions
> > >
What do you need from me to get started?

We’ll need your content (text and images), branding assets (logo, colors), and a clear idea of your goals. Don’t worry, we guide you through every step.

Can you add features like contact forms or booking systems?

Yes, we can add custom features like contact forms, booking systems, and more. Just let us know what you need during the project planning phase.

Do you provide hosting and domain services?

We can help you choose the right hosting provider and set up your domain, but hosting and domain registration are typically billed separately from web design services.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Absolutely. We can refresh your current site’s design, improve performance, and make it more user-friendly while keeping your existing content and branding.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Yes! Every website we build is fully responsive, meaning it looks and works great on phones, tablets, and desktops.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on the size and functionality of your website. Simple websites start at a lower cost, while custom or e-commerce solutions cost more. We provide transparent quotes so you know exactly what you’re paying for.
